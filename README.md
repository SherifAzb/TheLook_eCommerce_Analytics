## 📌 Project Overview
![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/SherifAzb/TheLook_eCommerce_Analytics/blob/main/pics/Screenshot%202026-10-22%20180702.png)
An end-to-end data analytics case study on **TheLook eCommerce** dataset[cite: 2]. This project explores sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product profitability, and operational efficiency to uncover actionable business insights and drive data-backed decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Business Questions
- **Sales & Revenue Trends:** How is revenue growing across different product categories, customer segments, and geographical regions?
- **Customer Retention & Behavior:** What are the key drivers of repeat purchases, order frequency, and customer lifetime value (CLV)?
- **Order Fulfillment & Operations:** Where are the bottlenecks in shipping, returns, and order processing times?
- **Product Performance:** Which brands and items generate the highest profit margins vs. highest return rates?

## 🛠️ Methodology & Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Handled missing values, standardized date formats, removed duplicates, and validated data integrity.
2. **Data Modeling & Feature Engineering:** Built structured relationships, calculated custom KPIs (Profit Margin %, Return Rate, Delivery SLA, YoY Growth), and segmented customers.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Analyzed historical trends, seasonal spikes, and category-level distributions.
4. **Dashboarding & Reporting:** Designed interactive pivot tables, dynamic charts, and executive summaries for clear stakeholder storytelling.
